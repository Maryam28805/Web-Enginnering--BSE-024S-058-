Our story

ByteBazaar started in 2019 as a small counter on Tariq Road. We helped friends and classmates find the right laptop without paying extra.

Today we sell laptops, phones and accessories to students, freelancers and small offices across Karachi. Our team tests every device before it goes out.

We keep prices clear and listed. No hidden charges.
