Questions and answers

Quick answers before you order.

Are your products original?

Yes. All devices are brand new and sealed, and you get a bill with every order.

How does delivery work?

Orders placed before 5pm are delivered the same day in Karachi. Other cities take 2 to 4 working days.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery, bank transfer and Easypaisa or JazzCash.

What if my device has a problem?

Bring it to the shop within one year with your bill. We will repair or replace it.

Can I return a product?

Yes, within 7 days if it is unused and in the original box.
